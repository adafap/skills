# Primary Sources

Retrieved and checked on 2026-08-07. These sources support issue spotting only. Recheck the live page before stating a deadline or current legal rule.

## California at-will baseline

- California Labor Code section 2922: <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=2922.>
  - Employment without a specified term is generally terminable at the will of either party.
  - Do not convert this baseline into a conclusion that every reason for termination is lawful.

## Civil-rights discrimination, retaliation, leave, and accommodation

- California Civil Rights Department employment page: <https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/Employment/>
  - Covers employment discrimination and retaliation connected to protected characteristics and specified protected rights.
  - The page states that FEHA employment-discrimination coverage generally applies to employers with five or more employees, while workplace harassment coverage is broader.
  - The page states that an employment-discrimination complaint generally must be filed within three years of the alleged act. Verify the live rule and exceptions before relying on it.
- California Civil Rights Department retaliation fact sheet: <https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2025/07/Retaliation-Factsheet-English.pdf>
  - Use for retaliation elements, complaint route, and current agency guidance.

## Labor Commissioner retaliation routes

- California Labor Commissioner retaliation and discrimination complaint procedure: <https://dir.ca.gov/dlse/RetaliationComplaintProcedure.htm>
- Laws enforced by the Labor Commissioner that prohibit retaliation or discrimination: <https://dir.ca.gov/dlse/howtofilelinkcodesections.htm>
  - The procedure states that many DLSE retaliation complaints have a one-year limit, with material exceptions including shorter periods for some claims.
  - Health-and-safety retaliation may involve a separate federal OSHA deadline. Treat it as immediately urgent and verify the current federal route.

## Whistleblower protection

- California Labor Code section 1102.5: <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=1102.5.>
  - Addresses retaliation for certain disclosures, anticipated disclosures, testimony, and refusal to participate in conduct the worker reasonably believes would violate law.
  - Do not conclude that the statute applies without checking the disclosure, belief, decision-maker knowledge, timing, and causation facts.

## Federal discrimination timing

- U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission time limits: <https://www.eeoc.gov/time-limits-filing-charge>
  - The EEOC explains a general 180-day charge period and circumstances in which it may extend to 300 days.
  - Never assume the extended period applies. Verify the claim basis, location, agency coverage, and current page immediately.

## Source-use rules

- Prefer the exact agency or legislative page over law-firm summaries and search snippets.
- Name the agency and claim family beside each deadline.
- Preserve conflicts or uncertainty instead of choosing the more convenient deadline.
- Do not state that an agency route replaces legal advice or preserves every other claim.

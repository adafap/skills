## Dismissal challenge deadline

### Three-week rule

KSchG section 4 generally requires an employee who wants to challenge a written dismissal to file at the labor court within three weeks after receipt. The relevant event is receipt, not the date printed on the notice.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__4.html

KSchG section 7 generally treats a dismissal as effective from the outset if invalidity is not asserted in time, subject to the statutory exceptions.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__7.html

KSchG section 5 permits late admission only under narrow conditions. An application is generally required within two weeks after the obstacle ends, with an outer six-month limit measured from the missed deadline. Never tell a user that a missed deadline is harmless or automatically fatal; escalate immediately.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__5.html

### Calendar calculation

BGB sections 187 and 188 govern event-based period calculation. ZPO section 222 applies the BGB rules to procedural periods and moves an end date that falls on Saturday, Sunday, or a general public holiday to the next working day. Local public holidays depend on the relevant place and must be verified.

Official sources:

- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__187.html
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__188.html
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__193.html
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/zpo/__222.html

The calculator reports a nominal date and adjusts weekends plus explicitly supplied public holidays. It does not determine the competent court or independently know local holidays. Advise filing earlier.

## Severance entitlement and comparison formula

There is no general rule that every dismissal creates severance. The Federal Employment Agency describes severance as generally voluntary and notes that it can affect unemployment benefits.

Official source: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/das-muessen-sie-beachten/kuendigung-abfindung-freistellung

KSchG section 1a creates a specific route for an operational dismissal only when the notice states that the dismissal is based on urgent operational requirements and that the employee can claim severance by allowing the three-week filing period to expire. The claim arises after the filing period and notice period conditions are met without the employee filing the dismissal-protection action.

The statutory formula is 0.5 monthly earnings for each year of employment. A remaining service period of more than six months rounds up to a full year. KSchG section 1a refers to the monthly-earnings definition in KSchG section 10(3), which includes the money and non-cash benefits due for regular working time in the month the employment ends. A simple gross base-salary input may therefore be incomplete.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__1a.html

Monthly-earnings source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__10.html

Always distinguish:

- a potential KSchG section 1a claim;
- a social-plan or collective right;
- a contractual right;
- a voluntary employer offer;
- a negotiated or court settlement;
- a comparison scenario.

The 0.75 and 1.0 factors are comparison scenarios only. Market outcomes depend on facts, evidence, leverage, litigation goals, costs, time, and local practice.

## General dismissal protection

KSchG section 1 applies the general social-justification framework after employment in the same establishment or company has lasted longer than six months. It recognizes personal, conduct-related, and urgent operational grounds and contains social-selection rules for operational dismissals.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__1.html

KSchG section 23 contains the establishment-size scope rules. The ordinary modern threshold is more than ten employees, but part-time weighting and transitional treatment for employment beginning before 2004 can change the analysis. Sections 4 through 7 remain important even where much of the first section does not apply to a small establishment.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__23.html

Do not determine coverage from headcount alone. Obtain working hours, start dates, establishment structure, and any transitional facts.

## Written form and notice-period baseline

BGB section 623 requires written form for dismissal and termination agreements and excludes electronic form. Do not determine validity from a scan alone; preserve and review the original and signature facts.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__623.html

BGB section 622 provides the ordinary baseline:

- four weeks to the fifteenth or month-end under the general rule;
- two weeks during an agreed probation period, for no more than six months;
- employer notice increasing at two, five, eight, ten, twelve, fifteen, and twenty completed years;
- possible deviations through collective agreements and limited contractual rules.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__622.html

The calculator reports only this statutory employer baseline. Always review the contract, collective agreement, fixed-term rules, special statutes, and whether the dismissal is extraordinary.

## Job-seeker registration and ALG I

SGB III section 38 generally requires job-seeker registration no later than three months before employment ends. If fewer than three months lie between learning the end date and the end of employment, registration is required within three days. The duty remains even when continued employment is being asserted in court.

Official sources:

- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__38.html
- https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/ihre-schritte-wenn-sie-arbeitslos-werden/wie-sie-sich-arbeitsuchend-melden

Job-seeker registration is not the same as unemployment registration. Encourage immediate registration rather than waiting for the calculated date.

SGB III section 159 addresses benefit suspension periods, including voluntarily ending employment without an important reason, conduct that causes termination, and late job-seeker registration. A termination agreement or alleged misconduct can therefore require case-specific review. Do not predict the agency's decision.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__159.html

SGB III section 158 can postpone benefit payment where severance or similar compensation is connected to employment ending before the employer's ordinary notice period would have expired. This is distinct from a suspension period under section 159.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_3/__158.html

The Federal Employment Agency asks users to disclose the reason for unemployment and identifies termination agreements, settlement agreements, and conduct-related dismissal as potentially relevant.

Official source: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/arbeitslos-arbeit-finden/arbeitslosengeld/das-muessen-sie-beachten/kuendigung-abfindung-freistellung

## First-instance labor-court costs

ArbGG section 12a generally excludes reimbursement of a prevailing party's lawyer or representative costs in first-instance judgment proceedings. A user should therefore compare potential value with their own legal costs, insurance, union support, and available assistance.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/arbgg/__12a.html

Do not calculate legal fees without the necessary matter value, scope, agreement, and current fee rules.

## Works council and special protection

BetrVG section 102 requires the employer to hear the works council before every dismissal where a works council exists; a dismissal issued without that hearing is ineffective. Do not infer whether consultation was valid from the notice alone.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/betrvg/__102.html

Special-protection flags require urgent review:

- MuSchG section 17: pregnancy, qualifying miscarriage, and protected postpartum periods, with employer-knowledge and notification rules. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/muschg_2018/__17.html
- BEEG section 18: parental-leave dismissal protection and limited exceptions. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/beeg/__18.html
- SGB IX section 168: prior Integration Office consent for dismissal of a severely disabled person, subject to statutory exceptions including section 173. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_9_2018/__168.html and https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_9_2018/__173.html
- KSchG section 15: protection for works-council members, election bodies, candidates, and related roles. https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kschg/__15.html

Do not make the final protection determination. Record the exact status, dates, employer knowledge, approvals, and exceptions.

## Discrimination and retaliation

AGG section 15 includes a separate written two-month assertion period for specified compensation and damages claims, subject to its detailed trigger and any collective-agreement variation. A dismissal challenge can still have its own three-week route.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/agg/__15.html

HinSchG section 36 prohibits reprisals against qualifying reporting persons and contains a burden-of-proof rule when its conditions are met.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hinschg/__36.html

Escalate suspected discrimination or retaliation. Do not decide protected status, causation, or available remedies from a narrative alone.

## Lawyer referral boundary

BRAO section 49b restricts success-based fees and prohibits giving or receiving fees or other benefits for referral of legal mandates, subject to the statutory text and exceptions. Do not propose outcome-based referral compensation.

Official source: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/brao/__49b.html

Any commercial lawyer-directory or handoff model requires separate German professional-regulation review before launch.

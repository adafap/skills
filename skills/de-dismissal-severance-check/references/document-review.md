# Document Review Protocol

Use this protocol for dismissal notices, termination agreements, settlement offers, social plans, employment contracts, collective agreements, warnings, and related correspondence.

## Preserve evidence first

1. Keep the original file unchanged.
2. Record the filename, file type, page count, visible signatures, and legibility.
3. Record the date printed on the document separately from the user's receipt date.
4. Mark OCR-derived text as uncertain until checked against the rendered page.
5. Do not treat a filename, email subject, or isolated keyword as proof of document type or legal effect.

## Extracted fields

Capture each value with page and supporting text:

- sender and intended recipient;
- document date and receipt date;
- stated document type;
- ordinary or extraordinary termination wording;
- employer's stated reason;
- proposed employment end date;
- statutory, contractual, or collective notice reference;
- KSchG section 1a statement;
- severance amount, calculation basis, payment date, and conditions;
- paid release, revocability, holiday allocation, and time-credit allocation;
- bonus, commission, equity, pension, company car, and other benefits;
- reference wording and performance rating commitment;
- early-exit or acceleration clause;
- non-compete and compensation wording;
- confidentiality, non-disparagement, return-of-property, and cooperation duties;
- release, waiver, settlement, and unknown-claims language;
- unemployment-benefit or tax representations;
- signing deadline, cooling-off language, and revocation rights if any;
- signatures and authority of signatories;
- referenced annexes, policies, social plans, or collective agreements.

## Review rules

- Quote only the minimum text needed to support an extracted value.
- Separate what the document says from what the law may require.
- Mark missing annexes and incorporated documents as unresolved dependencies.
- Do not assume a release is enforceable or unenforceable without qualified review.
- Do not assume a stated tax or ALG I outcome is accurate.
- Do not infer that garden leave consumes holiday unless the wording and applicable law have been reviewed.
- Do not infer that a stated severance formula creates a claim.
- Do not tell the user to sign, reject, or edit a legal document.

## Comparison output

When multiple drafts exist, produce a field-by-field table with:

- field;
- old value;
- new value;
- financial or legal effect to review;
- evidence location;
- unresolved question.

Flag deleted clauses as well as added clauses. Preserve exact version dates.
